Choosing a video previews it on your device. Camera access starts only after you choose Use camera and approve the browser permission. Recording has a 30-second limit.

Create GIF uploads your video to our conversion server. Rust calls FFmpeg to create your GIF; the upload is deleted when the job finishes or fails. Generated GIFs remain on the server until its storage is cleared. Do not upload sensitive videos.

Anyone with a GIF's unique download URL can retrieve it. There are no accounts or private sharing controls.
