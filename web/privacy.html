Your moment,
from still to loop.

Choosing an image or video previews it on your device. Camera access starts only after you choose Use camera and approve the browser permission. Recording has a 30-second limit.

Create GIF uploads your video to our conversion server. Animate image uploads your still image and motion instruction. HyperFrames moves the original image, and Rust calls FFmpeg to encode the GIF. No generative image service receives your image. The upload and temporary frames are deleted when the job finishes or fails. Generated GIFs remain on the server until its storage is cleared. Do not upload sensitive media.

Image motion currently moves the whole picture. It cannot generate gestures or animate individual subjects. Pan and float preserve source pixels at original size. Zoom, rotation and an explicitly selected resize resample them. Every GIF is limited to 256 colors and binary transparency.

Anyone with a GIF's unique download URL can retrieve it. There are no accounts or private sharing controls. Motion job status expires after ten minutes; restarting the server also clears pending status.

The GPUI settings panel runs in your browser. If GPU rendering is unavailable, the custom values controls remain usable.
